## Motion sequence

1. Begin close to the existing still.
2. Dense charcoal dithering subtly shifts and reorganises.
3. Dots gradually resolve into cleaner ruled structures and ordered marks.
4. Page surfaces become progressively calmer and clearer.
5. Complete the transformation.
6. Hold on the resolved artwork.

The book itself should remain almost stationary. Permitted secondary movement: extremely subtle camera push; slight parallax from page depth; almost imperceptible paper movement; very gentle lighting evolution. No dramatic page turns, rotations, floating geometry or continuous animation. Keep printed texture attached to the page surface rather than regenerating random noise every frame. Under reduced-motion preferences, show the resolved still.

Do not improve the artwork by adding more things. Improve it through fidelity, depth, texture, lighting and motion restraint.
